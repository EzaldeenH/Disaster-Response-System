Our Mission

On the Ground When It Matters Most

Helping Hands is a volunteer-driven disaster response organization. Since 2018, we've worked alongside local communities to deliver emergency shelter, medical care, and essential supplies to families affected by natural disasters — from earthquakes to floods to hurricanes.

We believe aid should be fast, dignified, and direct. Every dollar raised goes toward resources on the ground, not overhead.
